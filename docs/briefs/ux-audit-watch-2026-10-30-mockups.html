Watch UX audit, before and after

Companion to ux-audit-watch-2026-10-30.md; item numbers refer to its ranked plan. Each dial is the 454 px face the CI renders use, drawn on the dial's 384 px reference canvas and scaled by 1.18, the same conversion the app makes on that face. Ring radii, strokes, gaps, band arcs and type sizes come from DialGeometry.kt and DialType.kt; colors from WearTheme.kt and DayAccentColors.kt. Band text runs on SVG text paths around the band annulus, as the app's curved layout does.

The "before" faces follow the three CI renders where they exist (overview, lifting, rest) and the state code where they do not. Data is the CI demo day: Day A of four, squat then a superset, two sets logged. The first overview pair keeps the demo's full lift name, as the render does; the other faces use the catalog short names the phone actually sends ("Squat", "Incline DB"). Type falls back to a condensed system face where Barlow Condensed is not installed, so widths are approximate.
