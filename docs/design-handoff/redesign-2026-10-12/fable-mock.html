strength.log, redesigned around the day

Revision 2, after the owner's review. The identity stays: near-black, the earth accents, condensed numerals. What changes is who carries the hierarchy: size, one accent object per screen, and one sentence from a quiet coach, instead of captions. Every element below is a Material 3 library component or a plain composition of them; nothing is hand-drawn. Screens use the real program from the Pixel walk-through on 12 October.

  • One accent object, one accent action. The day index and the button. Nothing else on a screen is coloured.
  • Words in sentences, numbers in condensed. No tracked-caps eyebrows, no dot-joined meta strings. Caps survive only as set notation (R1, TOP, B/O).
  • The list is a ledger. A set is a value and a checkbox. Editing happens in a sheet over the list, never in it.
  • The coach states a fact, or nothing. Returning after a gap, loads holding. Never advice, never a streak.
  • Built from the library. Scaffold, TopAppBar, NavigationBar, LazyColumn, ListItem, Checkbox, ModalBottomSheet, Button. The theme carries the identity, not custom components.
  • Nothing moves with scroll. Fixed bars, keyed rows, one-item content swaps. Motion only answers a tap.

Today and the workout

The day index, a letter on its accent, is the one expressive move. It is large on Today and the receipt, small in the workout bar, and filed beside each session in Log. The bottom navigation bar names the three places the app has.

Log, Plan, Settings

Log leads with the lifter's own sessions. Setup splits into Plan (the rotation, goals and training assumptions) and Settings (everything that is about the app). Administration screens use no day accent, so the accent keeps its meaning.
