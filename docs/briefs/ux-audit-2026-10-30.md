# UX audit, 2026-10-30 (second pass)

Run with the `ux-design` skill against the rewritten `DESIGN.md` (repo root), over the phone
app, the home-screen widget, the launcher icons and the theme. Kotlin paths are relative to
`app/src/main/kotlin/cloud/trotter/log/strength/`, resource paths to `app/src/main/res/`;
`data/`, `domain/` and test paths are from the repo root. Visual evidence: the CI store
screenshots in `.shots/phoneScreenshots/` (01 to 07, all rendered Dark). Contrast figures are
WCAG ratios computed from the hex values in `ui/theme/Color.kt` and `DayAccentColors`.

## Changes from the first pass

The first pass treated the October redesign, the August KEEP inventory and the July palette
decisions as settled, so it could only raise them as owner questions. With history recast as
input, eight of those questions become plan items with a recommendation: the two filled buttons
on the workout page (item 3), plates under the numeral (6), the volume chart (11), the glance
caps (23), the bespoke components (25), the wizard's finish label (17), Add and Swap from search
(30) and the cascade's caps (31). New work comes from the owner's emphasis: every color role
and contrast pair is now measured in both expressions, which surfaced failing error text, faint
text, outlines, raw accents on four of seven days and a Snackbar action in light mode (items 2
and 4), a status bar that ignores the in-app theme choice (8), no light review renders at all
(9), and M3 conformance findings on outline roles, the type scale's tracking, button shapes,
the background/surface split, focus state layers, motion tokens and adaptive navigation (items
18 to 20, 26, 27). The launcher icon was not in the first pass; it does not do the job
`DESIGN.md` gives it (24). Dropped: every watch item (old 10 and 19, owner question 14, the watch
inventory), since this pass covers the phone only. `DESIGN.md` resolves no first-pass item
outright, though it now names the `primary` problem and the error contrast as known issues,
which item 1 and item 4 answer. One first-pass claim was wrong: the cardio activity strings at
`strings.xml:616-625` are used (`ui/day/CardioPresentation.kt:64`); the hard-coded caps are the
cardio mode names in `ui/log/LogScreenBuilder.kt:58-63`.

## Users and jobs

`DESIGN.md`'s job sentences hold for every phone surface with three exceptions. Setup carries
two jobs (training inputs and preferences), which `DESIGN.md` already records. The Log carries
history plus Health Connect status and the bodyweight prompt; item 11 and item 12 give those a
home. The day launcher icon's job is "show today's letter", and it shows a barbell (item 24).

## Plan, ranked by how much each change raises the user's chance of success

### 1. Color roles: a day-scoped primary on day surfaces, neutral ink elsewhere

- Surface: every screen.
- Today: `ui/theme/Theme.kt:64,123` set `primary = dayAccent(0)`, and `inversePrimary` and
  `primaryFixed` follow (`:68,94,127,145`); `tertiary` is Day D (`:73,132`). Every stock control
  that reads `primary` is Day A terracotta on every day: "Lift 1 of 4"
  (`ui/day/DayScreen.kt:369-374`), "All sets" (`ui/day/ExercisePage.kt:341-346`), the rest pill's
  time and Skip (`DayScreen.kt:717-742`), the finished lift's checkbox (`ExercisePage.kt:103`,
  orange in 05-lift-done), All sets checkboxes (`ui/day/AllSetsSheet.kt:90-96`), cardio buttons
  (`ui/day/CardioPresentation.kt:189,193,197`), the Plans FAB (`ui/plan/PlansScreen.kt:62`),
  dialog confirms in Log and Plan (`ui/log/LogScreen.kt:191-194`, `ui/plan/PlanScreen.kt:94-96`)
  and the note sheet's Done (`ui/components/NoteSheet.kt:41-44`). Admin screens hard-code Day A
  as well: `ui/components/SelectionCard.kt:62-65`, `ui/components/SwitchToggle.kt:58`,
  `ui/backup/BackupScreen.kt:86`, `ui/setup/SetupScreen.kt:90`, `ui/wizard/WizardScreen.kt:80`,
  `ui/components/AuthoredStates.kt:146,161`. As text, Day A is 3.79:1 on `#0D0D0F`, 3.52:1 on
  cards and 4.46:1 on paper. Color also misleads the other way: `SheetButton` defaults to Done
  green (`ui/day/DayEditSheet.kt:952`) and the weight-swap and partial-finish dialogs confirm in
  green (`DayScreen.kt:594,622`), so Add, Edit, Save and Switch read as completed work.
- Rule: color means one thing; accessibility floor; M3 color roles by meaning.
- Change: build the scheme per scope. Today, the workout, the receipt and the cascade wrap their
  content in `MaterialTheme(colorScheme = …)` derived for the day: `primary` is the day's
  derived foreground (`accentEmphasis(i)`, so text, checkboxes and focus are legible), its ink as
  `onPrimary`, the raw accent as `primaryContainer` with `onDayAccent(i)` on it, and the opposite
  expression's foreground as `inversePrimary`. That matches M3's tonal relationship in both
  expressions (in dark, `primary` lighter than its container; in light, darker). The three hero
  buttons (Start, Log, next lift) take `primaryContainer` colors at their call sites. Everywhere
  else `primary` is neutral ink (`onSurface`, with the ground as `onPrimary`) and `tertiary` is
  neutral too. `SheetButton` and dialog confirms use neutral for ordinary actions and `error`
  for destructive ones. `SelectionCard` and `SwitchToggle` lose `accentDayIndex` (or go, item 25).
- History: #40 (2026-07-06) set `primary` to Day A because screens drew their own controls; the
  August migration removed that premise, as `DESIGN.md` notes. The wear-dial-v3 entry (#116)
  made disc actions neutral white because "controls borrowing the accent mixed identity with
  action"; the same reason argues for neutral ink off the day surfaces. This answers
  `DESIGN.md` open question 2.
- Cost: M. Risk: low to medium. `ThemeCompletenessTest` and the M3 guardrail test change, and
  every screenshot changes.

## Decisions only the owner can make

Each carries a recommendation.

1. Dynamic color (`DESIGN.md` open question 3). Recommend none. The accent is information
   (which day it is), shared with the watch, the widget and the launcher; wallpaper colors
   would make `primary` mean nothing in particular, and every contrast pair in item 4 would
   have to hold for palettes nobody has seen. Neutral-only dynamic color adds a setting for a
   tint the user cannot name.
2. The launcher icon (item 24): the day letter on the day aliases, and does the store icon
   change with them or keep the barbell?
3. The share card. Recommend it stays dark: it is an image that leaves the phone, and the
   `light-theme.md` reason (a designed artifact) still holds. Its caps lines
   (`ui/log/share/ShareCardContent.kt:66,76,90`): sentence case to match the receipt, or caps as
   the public face?
4. Distance units: meters only, or kilometers and miles too (`DESIGN.md` open question 12)?
5. Keep screen on defaults off
   (`data/src/main/kotlin/cloud/trotter/log/strength/data/FullSnapshot.kt:42`) and lives in the
   workout's overflow. Default it on during a workout?
6. Timed holds on the phone record the prescription without a clock. Time them like timed-rep
   events?
7. Custom exercises: edit and delete before production? Save has no error state
   (`ui/customexercise/CustomExerciseViewModel.kt:181-202`).
8. Authored plans: give them emphasis and cardio settings, or hide those controls for good
   (item 13 hides them for now)?
9. The wizard's finish (item 17): "Use this plan" that lands on Today, or "Start day A" that
   opens the workout?
10. The bodyweight prompt, once Health Connect leaves the Log: Plan, Settings, or Today?
11. Tablets: in scope for v1? Item 26 covers landscape phones and foldables only.
