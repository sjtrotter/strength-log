strength.log is a strength-training tracker that keeps your data on your phone. No account, no ads, no internet permission. You pay once.

A short setup wizard asks about your training emphasis, how many days a week you want (two to six), your split, anchor lifts, equipment, and a few things about you: bodyweight, age and experience. From that it builds a rotation with calculated goal loads for the main lifts. Goals are read-only and change when your bodyweight, age or level does.

The workout screen shows one lift at a time. The set in front of you is large, with its goal and what you did last time, and one button logs it. The rest timer starts itself. Edit a set by stepping the numbers or typing them, with a plate-loading line for barbell lifts. Supersets, swaps, notes, reps-only and timed exercises are all there. Edit the top set and the warm-ups and back-off follow it. Beat your best top set when you finish a day and you get a short celebration.

Days advance through the rotation only when you finish them. A missed day just means the same day is still next.

The Wear OS app is a companion to the phone app. Log every set from your wrist: the prescribed set on the dial, a tap to log it, the crown to adjust, rest counting down with haptics. If the phone is out of reach, edits queue on the watch and sync when it reconnects, without doubling anything. You finish the day on the phone.

Cardio finishers, easy zone 2, a tempo run or intervals, are timed on the phone or the watch and land in the same history.

Your data is yours to take: a full JSON backup, manual or automatic to a folder you choose, CSV export in Strong's layout, import from Strong and Hevy CSVs, optional Health Connect, and a share card drawn on your device.

Built for people who want a log, not a feed.
