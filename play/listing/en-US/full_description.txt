strength.log is a strength-training tracker that keeps your data on your phone. No account, no ads, no network permission. You pay once.

A short setup wizard builds a maintenance-oriented plan from your bodyweight and age: two to six training days in a rotation, each with calculated goal loads for the main lifts. You log against those goals with as few taps as possible: one lift at a time, the set in front of you, one button to log it. The rest timer starts itself.

Workouts advance through the rotation as you finish them. A missed day shifts the plan; it never skips a muscle. Hit your numbers and the next goals cascade.

The Wear OS app runs a whole session from your wrist: the prescribed set on the dial, a tap to log it, the rest counting down. It works offline and syncs back when the phone is near, without doubling anything.

Cardio finishers, zone work, tempo or intervals, are timed on either device and land in the same journal.

Your data is yours to take: a full JSON backup, manual or automatic to a folder you choose, CSV export and import compatible with Strong, Health Connect integration, and a shareable workout card.

Built for people who want a log, not a feed.
