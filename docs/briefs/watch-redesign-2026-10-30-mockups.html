Watch redesign, mockups

Companion to watch-redesign-2026-10-30.md, revised after council rounds 1 and 2. Every face is the 454 px CI watch at density 2.0 (227 dp, 1 sp = 2 px) unless it says 192 dp. The old dial scaled its 384 px reference canvas to the face, 1.18 here; Material 3 sizes are in sp instead, so a 384 px Pixel Watch at the same density draws the same text at the same size, taking 1.18 times more of its face. The 192 dp section shows what that costs. Wear Material 3 proportions are approximate: TimeText curved at the top, the EdgeButton’s flat top 116 px above the bottom with its bottom following the screen edge, CompactButton chips 32 dp tall. No workout face scrolls; the lists are separate pages. The “today” drawings are copied from ux-audit-watch-2026-10-30-mockups.html. Data is the CI demo: day A of four, squat then a superset, two sets logged. Barlow Condensed loads from Google Fonts and falls back to a condensed system face, so widths are approximate.

Today and the redesign, side by side

Overview

Sixteen things to parse become five. The program’s days, their 9 sp labels and the lift ring leave; “Day A” says which day in one word. The lift names are a chip that opens the lifts page, and the one action is a labeled EdgeButton in the day’s bright accent.

A set

Today a set takes two faces and two taps, and the 60 px load only appears on the second, under the bar. The redesign shows the load at 120 px while the lifter is loading the bar; one press, armed after 0.4 s and released, logs it, so a brush or a doubled tap writes nothing. The face never scrolls; the lift chip opens the lift page.

Rest

The draining ring is visible (today it is painted under the disc), the next set’s position is back, and Undo for the set just logged sits at the top. The button skips the rest and never logs, so a doubled tap cannot write a set.

Timed hold

Today’s face has no render; this is drawn from DialState.kt:490-515, and its filling clock ring does not show. The redesign fills the edge ring toward the goal and logs itself at the goal. Stopping early takes a one-second press, so a wrist pressed against the button in a plank does nothing.

The densest faces at 192 dp

A 384 px Pixel Watch 2 at density 2.0, at font scale 1.0 and 1.3. System-font text grows with the scale; the hero numeral takes whatever height is left, down to its 36 sp floor. On this size the face keeps one detail line, chosen by the priority in the brief; the line it drops is on the lift page, and the rest face showed the next set’s position. At 1.3 the button label wraps to two lines, so the hold instruction survives at every scale. Hero sizes are given under each face.
