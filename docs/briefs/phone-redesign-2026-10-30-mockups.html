Phone redesign, mockups

Companion to phone-redesign-2026-10-30.md. Every phone is 360 × 800 dp drawn 1:1 in px, with a 24 dp status bar and a 24 dp gesture bar; 1 sp = 1 px at the default font scale. The “today” drawings follow the CI renders in .shots/phoneScreenshots/ (360 × 720 dp, no status bar), moved down under a status bar with the bottom chrome anchored to the taller frame; Log, Plan and Setup have no render and are drawn from their composables. Material 3 proportions are approximate: 64 dp small top app bars, an 80 dp navigation bar plus the gesture inset, 56 and 64 dp buttons, 56 and 72 dp list items, 28 dp sheet corners. Demo data is the CI demo (Four-day strength, day A Squat focus, the spec §11 squat and bench numbers for the reference lifter) plus three invented days and dates around Friday, Oct 30, 2026. Barlow Condensed loads from Google Fonts and falls back to a condensed system face, so widths are approximate.

The set loop

When a rest ends the band collapses, one buzz plays and Log fills again; the plain set screen is the signal. With the rest timer off, the band holds only Undo for 10 s. Undo unticks the set, clears its stamps, cancels its rest and returns to it.
